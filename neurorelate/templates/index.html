Why an RDBMS for RAG?

Combining the strict relational schema guarantees of SQL with the semantic capabilities of AI retrieval.

Relational Chunking & Cascade

Documents and their semantic chunks are stored in normalized tables linked with FOREIGN KEY ... ON DELETE CASCADE, preventing orphaned chunks when files are modified or deleted.

Hybrid Retrieval Engine

Multi-stage retrieval evaluates exact phrases, TF-IDF keyword density, and token overlap coverage across chunks, returning rank-ordered context within milliseconds.

Cited & Grounded Answers

Every AI-generated answer strictly references the originating document filename and chunk number, providing full provenance and preventing hallucinations in enterprise workflows.
